# Cub Scout Pack 127 Calendar Specification

## Overview
This document specifies how to create the annual calendar for Cub Scout Pack 127.
The pack calendar is generated at the start of each summer and should follow the
example on the first page of
<https://docs.google.com/document/d/10E-W_T7-Wz04ttZoq9v8EV6A43I9H8bmotKo5GuqHfw/edit?pli=1&tab=t.0>

The calendar being generated now is for the **2026-2027** school year and the
following summer.

## Yearly Maintenance
A few inputs must be refreshed by a human before each year's calendar is generated:

- **ACPS school calendar** — used to find off weeks (holidays/closures). See
  <https://www.alachuaschools.net/o/acps/page/calendars>. Confirm the first and
  last student days and all breaks for the target school year.
- **Council "Ideal Year of Scouting" (IYOS) calendar** — the North Florida
  Council publishes this each year as an **image-only PDF**, so a human must
  download it, run OCR on it, and update the copy in this repository so its
  dates can be read. The current OCR'd copy is
  `cub 26.27 ideal year of scouting OCR.pdf`; the source is at
  <https://www.nfcscouting.org/files/48722/ideal-year-of-scouting-2026-2027-cub-scouts>
  and the always-current council event list is at
  <https://www.nfcscouting.org/calendar>.
- **Council event links** (popcorn, Frontier, BALOO, etc.) — verify each year,
  as URLs and dates change.

## Output Format
- Generate the calendar as a **docx** file.
- Cover the 2026-2027 school year and the following summer.
- Filename: the school year, then " Pack 127 Calendar", plus the `.docx`
  extension (e.g., `2026-2027 Pack 127 Calendar.docx`).
- Title: the school year, then " Pack 127 Calendar".
- Two columns per page; text flows from column to column across as many pages as
  required.
- Each entry is a single event. Keep the majority of event names in the format
  **Day of Month, Activity, Location**.
- Recognize the start of each new month, either by putting the month name on its
  own line in bold, or by putting a month abbreviation in-line with an event.
  The first and last month of the calendar, and every December and January,
  should include the year. Examples:
  - Month and day on separate lines: **December 2026** / **3** Den Meeting (Cabin)
  - Month and day on the same line: **Mar 2** Pack Meeting (Cabin)
- If an event needs to wrap onto another line for the description to be
  meaningful, just do it.

## Event Types
There are four kinds of events:

- **Pack Meetings** — all families meet together as a pack.
- **Den Meetings** — the pack splits up by grade into dens.
- **Committee Meetings** — the adult leadership meeting.
- **Special Events** — everything else. Specific lists are in the sections below.

## Weekly Meeting Schedule
Every Wednesday during the school year at 6 pm should be a Pack Meeting or a Den
Meeting. There are two exceptions:

1. A Special Event.
2. An off week.

### Off Weeks
An off week happens when there is a holiday or a recognized holiday week that
closes Alachua County Public Schools (see the ACPS calendar linked above). If a
week is an off week, note it specifically as **OFF** rather than just skipping
it. Thanksgiving is an off week.

### August and September (Pre-Sign-Up)
In August and September the recruiting events, called SUNs or Sign-Up Nights,
have not yet finished and not all scouts are present. If dens met separately to
do required advancement, scouts who have not yet signed up would miss
requirements — so these meetings should be pack-wide and work on elective
activities.

### Scheduling Considerations
- Fall recruiting creates organized chaos through October, and the district
  schedules major recruiting events in mid-October, so keep early-fall
  programming simple.
- Shorter fall daylight complicates distant campouts.
- Family attendance drops off from Veterans Day through Christmas.
- Families re-engage in the spring, which also brings more daylight and more
  mature, tool-trained scouts — the reason the Pinewood Derby stays in the spring.
- **Do not schedule any pack activity on the weekend of the Lubee Florida Bat
  Festival — Saturday, October 24, 2026** (<https://www.lubee.org/flbatfestival>).
  This is why the fall campout avoids that weekend and the pack skips the
  district Monster Bash family camp (Oct 23-25, 2026); see Special Events.

## Pack Meetings
Pack Meetings are held no more than once per month and have everyone together.
On Page 1 the location and time can simply read "(Cabin 6pm)", but in the details
these meetings need everyone together in Fellowship Hall (FH) — the only venue
large enough for the entire pack.

## Committee Meetings
Committee meetings are one-hour meetings on the last Thursday of the month at
9 pm on Zoom. They are always on a non-Wednesday night so they never fall on the
same night as a pack or den meeting. If the last Thursday is a holiday, or falls
in a week with no Wednesday meeting, move the committee meeting forward or
backward by the smallest whole number of weeks needed to make it work.

## Special Events
Special events need not be on Wednesday nights. The pack holds three annual pack
campouts (fall, winter, and spring) and attends one district campout each year.
A list follows.

0. **Kickoff.** The season kickoff is the **Back to the Pack** event in August
   (see Recruitment). A late-summer recruitment party (e.g., a foam party) may
   precede it.
1. **Popcorn Kickoff Party.** A council event. The 2026 party is **Saturday,
   August 1, 2026, 10am-2pm at St. Johns River Base (SJRB), Orange Park**
   (<https://www.nfcscouting.org/2026popcorn>). Scan the council website or Gmail
   for each year's party. The council popcorn sale runs August-December.
2. **Frontier Shooting Day** (or similarly renamed shooting-sports event) at
   Camp Shands. The 2026 date is **September 19, 2026**
   (<https://www.nfcscouting.org/frontier>).
3. **BALOO Training** (adult leaders). Council options for this cycle are
   **November 7-8, 2026** and **May 7-8, 2027**
   (<https://www.nfcscouting.org/baloomarion>).
4. **Fall Campout.** Traditionally the Friday-to-Sunday weekend closest to
   Halloween that does not interfere with trick-or-treat night. For 2026-2027,
   also avoid the Lubee Bat Festival weekend (Oct 24, 2026); the weekend of
   **October 16-18, 2026** works and aligns with the council's Spookoree family
   camp. Keep it close to home with a one-night-mandatory / two-night-optional
   format (for example, Troy Springs with a ranger partnership).
5. **Winter Campout (district).** The pack's preferred district campout is the
   **Medieval Faire family camp at Camp Shands, February 5-7, 2027** — chosen
   over the fall Monster Bash (Oct 23-25, 2026) because fall recruiting keeps the
   pack too busy and that weekend is the Lubee Bat Festival.
6. **Spring Campout.** A spring camping event at Camp Shands — for example the
   council **Family Camp, April 23-25, 2027** (formerly referred to as BUC TUOCS).
7. **Pinewood Derby.** Occurs on a Saturday and Sunday. Pick the Saturday and
   Sunday after Valentine's Day and move it if needed. It should be at least two
   weeks before the district Pinewood Derby (the North Florida Council / Five
   Rivers district derbies are in April 2027). Hold 2 or 3 Pack Meetings called
   Build Nights beforehand where scouts work on their cars.
8. **Blue & Gold Banquet.** A Pack Meeting where the AOL Den finishes Cub Scouts
   and crosses over into Scouts BSA. It **must fall after the Pinewood Derby** so
   the AOLs complete the Race Time elective (earned at the Derby) before they
   cross over; do not move it before the Derby. Target late February or March,
   and no later than April, allowing the AOLs time to complete all mandatory rank
   requirements plus a make-up night.
9. **Troop Visits.** These apply only to the AOL Den. Each AOL scout must visit a
   Scouts BSA troop on their timetable. Note this in the calendar details, not on
   the top page, as it is arranged by the AOL Den Leader with the troops.
10. **Rain Gutter Regatta.** A fall recruitment activity that complements the
    spring Pinewood Derby. It can be completed in about an hour with minimal tools
    (foam, pool noodles, wooden skewers) and is simple enough for kindergartners
    to do independently. Hold it at the fall pack campout or as a standalone
    event. Materials are available from Troop 125 and James's storage.

## Program Recommendations
The calendar should have additional pages with the program recommendation — the
exact activity that the pack or each den should be doing at each meeting.

### Program Details
Program details for each den can be found at
<https://www.scouting.org/programs/cub-scouts/adventures/>.

Suggest an agenda and a location for each den for each den meeting. Use these
locations:

1. **Cabin.** Each den should get some meetings in the cabin. Anything involving
   tools, like Baloo the Builder, works best in the cabin.
2. **Playground.** There is a playground, Roper Park, available.
3. **Fellowship Hall.** A large indoor meeting space with a kitchen, so anything
   involving cooking or food works best here.
4. **The Chapel.** A medium indoor meeting space.
5. **Upstairs.** A classroom in Epworth Hall.
6. **Bethany Room in Bell Hall.** A classroom-size indoor space.

### Arrow of Light & Troop Integration
The AOL Den needs two elective adventures (the Pinewood Derby counts as one),
must complete one campout (at least one night in their own tent), and must attend
a troop activity or open house (troops typically hold open houses in
August/September). The AOL Den Leader coordinates summer troop visits (for
example with Troop 125); partner troops include 125, 84, and 21. Den chief youth
from these troops may lead adventure activities and are expected to be in full
Class A uniform.

### Program Constraints
1. The program details offer multiple paths to complete each activity, with
   suggested times. Allocate at most 30-40 minutes of program time per meeting.
2. Early-fall meetings should focus on outdoor requirements like physical fitness
   until the clocks fall back. Before the clock change, the playground has sun
   during 6 pm meetings and is available; after the clock change it is dark and
   unavailable until after the spring-forward date, when outdoor activities at
   the playground resume.
3. Bobcat activities can be done first, as part of pack-wide activities.
4. Combine one moving activity into each den meeting. The Webelos and AOLs can do
   all desk work, but the younger kids need to move.
5. Each den has mandatory adventures and some number of elective adventures.
   Make sure the AOLs have time for two elective adventures. One AOL elective
   should be Knife Safety; the other can be Race Time, covered by the Pinewood
   Derby.
6. Do the Webelos Chef's Knife adventure. It should happen in the spring.
7. Bears should do the Whittling and Baloo the Builder adventures. Whittling
   should happen in the spring.
   8. Do not plan anything for Mother's Day weekend.
