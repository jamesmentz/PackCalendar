# Cub Scout Pack 127 Calendar Specification

## Overview
This document specifies the creation of a calendar for Cub Scout Pack 127. The pack calendar should be generated at the start of each summer and should follow the example on the first page of
<https://docs.google.com/document/d/10E-W_T7-Wz04ttZoq9v8EV6A43I9H8bmotKo5GuqHfw/edit?pli=1&tab=t.0>

## Program Recommendations
The Calendar should have additional paged with the program recommendation. This is the exact activity that the pack or each den should be doing at each meeting.

## Calendar Details

### Formatting
Generate the calendar as a docx file.  
This calendar should cover the 2025-2026 Schoolyear and the following summer.  
The Calendar filename should be the schoolyear and the words " Pack 127 Calendar" plus the docx extension.  
The Calendar title should be the schoolyear and the words " Pack 127 Calendar".  
The Calendar should have two columns per page.  
The text should flow from column to column, using a many pages as required.  
Each entry should be a single event. Try to keep the majority of the event names to the format  
Day of Month, Activity, Location.  
The start of each new month should be recognized, either by putting the name of the month on its own line in bold or by putting an abbreviation for the month in-line with an event. The first and last month of the calendar, and December and January, should include the year.  
Example of month and day on separate lines  
** December 2025 **  
** 3 ** Den Meeting (Cabin) 
Example of month and day on the same line  
**Mar 2** Pack Meeting (Cabin)  
If an event needs to wrap onto a different line for the description to be meaningful, just do it.  

### Dates
There are five kinds of events: Pack Meetings, Den Meetings, Committee Meetings, and Special Events.  
Pack Meetings are where all families meet together as a pack.  
Den Meetings are where the the pack splits up by grade into Dens.  
Special Events are everything else. A specific list of Special Events will be provided in its own section.  
Every Wednesday during the schoolyear at 6 pm should be a Pack Meeting or a Den meeting. There are two exceptions,  
1. A Sepcial Event
2. An off week

#### Off Weeks
Off weeks should happen if there is a Holiday or a recognized Holiday week that closes Alachua Country Public Schools. The ACPS calendar is here:  
<https://www.alachuaschools.net/o/acps/page/calendars>
If a week is an off week is should be specifically noted as OFF, not just skipped. Thanksgiving is an off week.  

#### Pack Meetings
Pack Meetings should be held no more than once per month. Pack meetings have everyone together. The location and time can simply be "(Cabin 6pm)" on Page 1 but in the details these meetings need everyone together in Fellowship Hall, FH, the only venue large enough for the entire pack to meet together.

The Exception is August and September. These months the recruiting events, called SUNs or Sign-Up  Nights, have not yet occured and all the scouts are not here. Den Meeting would do required advancement and the scouts that have not yet signed up would miss requirements, so those meetings should be pack-wide and work on elective activities.

## Committee Meetings
Committee meetings are one hour meetings the last Thursday of the month at 9pm on Zoom. If that is a holiday or week with no Wednesday meeting then move the committee meeting forward or backward the smallest integer number of weeks needed to make it work.

## Special Events

Special events need not be on Wednesday nights. A list follows.  
0. A kickoff event. This is typically a party at the end of summer before school starts. There should be a scouting party 
1. Popcorn kickoff party. This is a council event. Scan the council website or gmail for this year's party. The 2025 event was at <https://www.nfcscouting.org/content/130287/Popcorn-Kickoff-Party>
2.  Frontier Day or similar renamed shooting sports event at Camp Shands. The 2026 version is at <https://www.nfcscouting.org/frontier>
3.  BALOO Training. An event link is at <https://www.nfcscouting.org/baloomarion>
4.  Fall Campout. This is traditionally the Friday to Sunday weekend closest to Halloween that does not interfere with trick-or-treat night.
5.  Winter Campout. Pick a weekend and mark it TBD. The committee has to fill in the details.
6.  BUC TUOCS at Camp Shands or similar Spring Camping event.
7.  Pinewood Derby. This event occurs on a Saturday and Sunday. Pick the Saturday and Sunday after Valentine's day and move it if needed. It should be at least two weeks before the Five Rivers Pinewood Derby District Event. There should be 2 or 3 Pack Meetings called Build Nights where scouts can work on their cars before the Pinewood Derby.
8.  Blue & Gold Banquet. This is a Pack Meeting where the AOL Den finished Cub Scouts and Crosses over into Scouts BSA. This should be no later than April. Try to move it as early as practical into March based on how the schedule allows the AOLs to complete all of the mandatory rank requirements along with time for a make-up night.
9.  Troop Visits. These only apply to the AOL Den. Each AOL Den needs to visit a Scouts BSA Den on their timetable. Worth noting in the calendar details, not the top page, as this needs to be arranged by the AOL Den Leader with the Troops.

## Program Details
Program Details for each Den can be found at 
<https://www.scouting.org/programs/cub-scouts/adventures/>  

Please suggest an Agenda and Location for each Den for each Den meeting. Use these Locations.
1. Cabin. Each Den should get some meetings in the cabin. Anything involving tools, like Balloo the Builder, works best in the cabin.
2. Playground. There is a playground, Roper Park, available.
3. Fellowship Hall. A large indoor meeting space. There is a kitchen so anything involving cooking or food works best in here.
4. The Chapel. A medium indoor meeting space.
5. Upstairs. Classroom in Epworth Hall.
6. Bethany Room in Bell Hall. Classroom size indoor space.

## Program Constraints
1. The Program details have multiple paths to complete each activity and suggested times. Allocate 30-40 minutes of program time per meeting at the most.
2. Early fall meetings should focus on outdoor requirements like physical fitness until the clocks fall back. Before the clock change the playground has sun durign 6 pm meetings and is available. After the clock change the playground is dark and unavailable until after the Spring Forward date when we can do outdoor things at the playground again.
3. Bobcat activities can be done first as part of packwide activities.
4. Try to combine one moving activity into each Den meeting. The webelos and AOLs can do all desk work, the younger kids need to move.
5. Note that each Den has Mandatory adventures and some number of elective adventures. Make sure the AOLs have time for two elective adventures. One AOL Elective adventure should be Knife Safety. The oither can be Race Time and will be covered by the Pinewood Derby.
6. Do the Webelos Chef's Knife Adventure.'It should happen in the Spring.
7. Bears should do the Whittling and Baloo the Builder Adventure. Whittling should happen in the Spring.
